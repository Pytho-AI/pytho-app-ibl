Operation Overview

This document outlines the planning considerations and evaluation criteria for a military operation. The core objective is to establish a secure environment for humanitarian relief efforts and mitigate risks to forces and the mission.

Evaluation Criteria

The following criteria will be used to assess different Courses of Action (COAs).

Criteria Definition Unit of Measure Benchmark Scoring Scale (0-3)
Sustainable Permissive Environment COA creates a sustainable permissive security environment. Source of forces required. HN provided security, local is better. 0 - Permissive environment is not established; 1 - U.S. forces present; 2 - HN military forces present; 3 - Sustainable by HN law enforcement.
Speed of Humanitarian Assistance COA achieves quick removal of AFTHA and food delivery. Number of days (relative to C-Day). C+31 - C+45 days; less days is better. 0 - More than C+61 days; 1 - C+46 - C+60; 2 - C+31 - C+45; 3 - Less than C+30 days.
Risk to JTF Mission Level of acceptable risk to military mission. Level of residual risk remaining. Moderate residual risk level (lower is better). 0 - High residual risk; 1 - Significant residual risk; 2 - Moderate residual risk; 3 - Low residual risk.
Risk to JTF Forces Level of acceptable risk to assigned JTF forces. Level of residual risk remaining. Moderate residual risk level (lower is better). 0 - High residual risk; 1 - Significant residual risk; 2 - Moderate residual risk; 3 - Low residual risk.
Risk of Chinese Intervention Risk of Chinese intervention across the DIME. Level of residual risk remaining. Low residual risk level (lower is better). 0 - High residual risk; 1 - Significant residual risk; 2 - Moderate residual risk; 3 - Low residual risk.
